Why we built this

Most platforms are good if you're already successful. They help you show off what you've done and connect with people who are also already doing well. That's not much use if you're still trying to get there.

Mericet is built for the people still trying. Innovators, early professionals, people willing to put in the work but haven't had the chance yet. I'm one of them too.

Here's what that means in practice: your profile works like a living resume that updates as you actually do things, not a form you filled in once. Real opportunities come to you as they show up, instead of once in a rare while. And when you post something worth seeing, people actually see it.

Mericet isn't promising — we're executing. No one has a full stop here. You learn and grow at any stage, at any place.

Five things every existing network gets wrong

Mericet exists because of these, in this order.

01

Powerful networks, zero help

Most professional networks are built for people who've already succeeded — to show off, not to help you grow. Connect with someone doing well and they'll forget you exist by the next scroll.

02

No real opportunities

Jobs. Network. Workspace. Platforms hand these out, but almost nobody turns them into something real. Give someone an actual opportunity to prove themselves, and they'll take it.

03

A profile that can't prove itself

Nobody can verify what you write about yourself online. Everyone performs professional — almost nobody can prove it. And you're stuck updating it by hand, forever.

04

Fifteen apps, one journey

Network on one app. Fix your profile on another. Chase opportunities on a third. Nothing owns your whole growth — so growth becomes tab-switching.

05

Built for attention, not ability

Cringe posts. Loud arguments. Every algorithm chasing dopamine, not value. The ideas that could actually move things forward never stood a chance.

What you actually get

Not a mission statement. What changes for you, specifically.

Mericet changes what gets rewarded. Real work gets noticed, not just loud opinions. Opportunities show up as they come in, instead of once in a rare while. Your profile grows because of what you actually do here, not because you filled in more fields.

None of that requires you to already be somebody. It requires you to actually be doing something — building, learning, trying. That's the only requirement.

The bigger goal is making that true for as many people as possible, not just the ones who already had a head start.
